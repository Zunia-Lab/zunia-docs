## 5. Fees

The wallet is free to install, with no premium tier and no token. Network fees are paid to validators / chains, not to Zunia. Sends, IBC transfers and staking carry no Zunia commission: you pay only the network fee.

Swaps made with the Swap screen of the Zunia browser extension carry a 0.5% commission, sent to Zunia. It is taken in the same transaction as the swap, in the token you sell, and shown before you sign. For a cross-chain swap (funds on another chain, swapped on Osmosis), the commission is taken on the starting chain when the transfer is sent. If the swap then fails on Osmosis, the amount swapped comes back; the commission does not. Swaps made on another website or dApp and only signed with Zunia carry no Zunia commission.
