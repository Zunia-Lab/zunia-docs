# Staking and governance

Home → **Stake** opens Earn: validators, commission, your delegations and rewards.

- **Delegate** from a validator row.
- **Redelegate** and **undelegate** from a delegation. Unbonding time is the chain's (often 14 or 21 days).
- **Claim** rewards from Earn.

Each action has its own review screen (amount, fee, validator). Sign on the device.

Home → menu → **Governance** lists proposals on the selected network. You can vote yes / no / abstain / no-with-veto. The wallet does not deposit for you.

Staking parameters (minimum, unbonding, APR figures) come from the chain. Zunia takes no cut of rewards.
