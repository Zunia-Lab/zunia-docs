# Staking and governance

Home → **Stake** opens Earn: validators, commission, your delegations and rewards.

- **Stake** with a validator from its row.
- **Unstake** from a delegation (extension only). Unbonding time is the chain's (often 14 or 21 days).
- **Claim rewards** from Earn.

Neither app redelegates. To move stake, unstake, wait out the unbonding time, then stake again.

Each action has its own review screen (amount, fee, validator). Sign on the device.

Home → menu → **Governance** lists proposals on the selected network. You can vote **Yes**, **No**, **Veto** (no with veto) or **Abstain**. The wallet does not deposit for you.

Staking parameters (minimum, unbonding, APR figures) come from the chain. Zunia takes no cut of rewards.
