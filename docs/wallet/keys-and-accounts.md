# Keys and accounts

Zunia is non-custodial. Your seed phrase and private keys never leave your device.

## Accounts

- One seed phrase can derive multiple accounts (HD wallet, BIP-39 / BIP-44)
- Cosmos chains use coin type 118 by default (coin type 60 for EVM-compatible chains)
- Each account has a bech32 address per connected chain

## Backup

Write down your 12 or 24-word recovery phrase and store it offline. Zunia cannot recover a lost phrase.

## Hardware wallets

Ledger and Keystone support is planned. Track progress in [zunia-extension](https://github.com/Zunia-Lab/zunia-extension) releases.
